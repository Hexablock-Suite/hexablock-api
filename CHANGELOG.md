# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are documented in this file. The format follows [Keep a Changelog 1.1.0](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Each sprint of the plan in [`hexablock-app/docs/ROADMAP.md`](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md) closes with a version section and a `v0.N.0` tag, the same tag as the app (rule R-sprint-release in `AGENTS.md`). The final delivery is `v1.0.0`.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The leaderboard endpoints (API-FR-03), both behind `JwtAuthGuard` (S3-09, a proposal on [#76](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/76)) ([#83](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/83)). `GET /leaderboard/{gameId}` answers `GlobalLeaderboardDto` with both global lists of the game: `highScores` (`rank`, `userId`, `highScore`) and `standings` (`rank`, `userId`, `stars`, `levelsCompleted`, `moves`, `elapsedMs`). `GET /leaderboard/{gameId}/levels/{levelId}` answers `LevelLeaderboardDto` with the `entries` of one level (`rank`, `userId`, `stars`, `moves`, `elapsedMs`). `GetLeaderboardUseCase` binds `UseCase`, and `AppModule` wraps it in `LoggingAspect`. It orders each list with `Score.isHigherThan()`, `Standing.isBetterThan()` or `LevelResult.isBetterThan()`, and gives each row a `rank`: 1 plus the rows that are strictly better, so a full tie shares a rank and the next rank skips (1, 1, 3). Tied rows come out by `userId` ascending through the new `UserId.comesBefore()`. A list holds only the players who have a result to rank, and no list has a page size. A broken `gameId` or `levelId` answers 400 and a missing token 401, with the `ErrorDto` body. `LeaderboardDtoMapper` translates the path and the answer. `openapi/openapi.json`, `docs/architecture/flows.md`, decisions 7 and 12 of `docs/architecture/classes.md` and four views with their PNG record the routes and the schemas.
- The leaderboard reader and the values that order the boards ([#82](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/82)). The `LeaderboardReader` port in `src/application/ports/` reads, for one `gameId`, the board of one level (`levelResults()`), the high scores of Lines and Merge (`highScores()`) and the standings of Fit and Sort (`standings()`), as `LeaderboardEntry<TResult>` values: the `UserId` of the player and the domain value that ranks the player. `DrizzleLeaderboardReader` implements it over the `progress` and `level_results` tables: it filters and sums, adds no order and names no table or column of `users`. The new value objects `LevelsCompleted` and `Standing` have factories that return a `Result`. `Standing.isBetterThan()` holds the global order of Fit and Sort (S3-03, a proposal on [#76](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/76)): more total stars, then more levels completed, then fewer total moves, then less total time. `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` records S3-03 next to DS-4, decision 2 of `docs/architecture/classes.md` drops «read model» and names the new types, and four views draw the change with their PNG. No HTTP route, table or migration changes: the use case and the endpoint come with #83.
- The level routes of API-FR-04 ([#81](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/81)). `GET /levels` has no guard and answers 200 with `{ "levels": LevelDto[] }`, every stored level by `gameId` and then by `levelId`. `PUT /levels` is behind `JwtAuthGuard`, takes the same body and answers 204: it is an idempotent batch, because an equal `version` passes (S3-10). `LevelDto` is one level of `docs/level-format.md`, field for field, and `openapi/openapi.json` gives its section as `FitSectionDto` or `SortSectionDto`; the export only adds schemas. A broken update answers 400 with the `ErrorDto` body, and the API checks it in two steps: first `LevelDtoMapper` checks the shape of the whole body, and each level with wrong fields or a wrong common value (`gameId`, `levelId`, `order`, `version` or `topology`) gets one detail, for its first problem; only when every level passes that step, `UpdateLevelsUseCase` checks the rules of each level (its section, A5, a `gameId` with no level files, then a lower `version` in its own save), and each level that breaks a rule gets one detail, for its first broken rule. Each detail names its level by its `gameId` and its `levelId`. A missing token answers 401, and a stored level that breaks a rule answers the 500 of an unexpected error. `GetLevelsUseCase` and `UpdateLevelsUseCase` bind `UseCase`. `UpdateLevelsUseCase` checks every level through `LevelValidators` before it saves any, then saves level by level, each in its own transaction, through `LevelRepository.update()`; a failed save keeps the levels saved before it, and a retry of the batch saves every level. Its failure is `InvalidLevels`, `{ kind: 'invalid-levels', problems }`, so `LoggingAspect` logs its kind, and `AppModule` wraps it. `LevelDtoMapper` builds the request and the answer. `AppModule` registers the Fit and Sort strategies once and binds `LEVEL_REPOSITORY` to `DrizzleLevelRepository`. Decisions 7, 11 and 12 of `docs/architecture/classes.md`, `flows.md` (routes, Q4) and S3-02 and S3-10 in `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`, as proposals for the team, follow the code. The level format, its fixtures and the strategies of `AppModule` use the game ids of the product, `hexa-fit` and `hexa-sort`, in place of `fit` and `sort`, so the levels and the progress of one game share its id; a level of the game `fit` now gets `game-without-levels`. Views 2.1, 2.3, 3.3, 3.5, slice 3 and the update levels sequence draw the built classes; extensibility view 1 already did. The domain surface gate passes with no new entry.

The DS-6 table of `docs/architecture/classes.md` lists the new field, and view 1.2 draws it in `FitSection`. No HTTP change and no code: the API has no level code yet ([#66](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/66)).
- Sign-up rejects a username that differs from a stored one only in letter case (`Ada` after `ada`) with the same 409 `Username is already taken` as an exact duplicate ([#62](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/62)). The username is still stored as typed, and sign-in still compares it as typed, so `ADA` does not sign in the stored `ada`. The migration `drizzle/0002_username_lower_unique.sql` adds a unique index on `lower(username)`, so two sign-ups at the same time cannot both pass. On a database that already holds two such names, the migration fails and keeps every row. The `UserRepository` port adds `isUsernameTaken()`, which ignores letter case, and the 409 description in `openapi/openapi.json` names the rule. The e2e test of #49 that expected two accounts now expects the 409.
- The username and password limits have one source ([#60](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/60)). The new generic value object `Range<T>` in `src/domain/shared/range.ts` keeps its bounds and answers `contains(value: T): boolean`; `Range.ofNumbers(min, max)` rejects `min > max` with a `RangeError`. The professor modeled the limits as a generic range with a contains method in the session of 2026-11-06. `src/domain/user` exports `USERNAME_LENGTH` (3 to 32) and `PASSWORD_LENGTH` (8 to 128), and the value objects, the register error messages and `CredentialsDto` read them. The password length now counts Unicode code points, not UTF-16 units, so four emoji fail the 8-character minimum (NIST SP 800-63B §5.1.1.2). Login rejects a password longer than 128 characters before the hash check, with the same 401 as a wrong password. `POST /auth/login` takes the new `LoginDto`, so `openapi/openapi.json` no longer publishes the registration limits for login; the register schema does not change.
- A synced level result no longer replaces the stored one. `PlayerProgress.recordLevel()` keeps the best result of each level by the order of DS-4, which the team closed on 2026-11-06: more stars wins, then fewer moves, then less time, and on a full tie the stored result stays. `LevelResult.isBetterThan()` holds the order. `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` closes DS-4 and drops the interim last-result sentence of DS-12; the class and flow docs say that the same order ranks the per-level leaderboard of Fit and Sort. The HTTP schema does not change ([#24](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/24)).
- `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` DS-6 adds `stars` to the Sort section of the level schema: a list of star thresholds, each one a `maxMoves` count. The team chose stars by moves for Sort on 2026-11-06 ([Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app#59](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/59)). The DS-6 table of `docs/architecture/classes.md` lists the new field, and view 1.2 draws it in `SortSection`. No HTTP change and no code: the API has no level code yet ([#57](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/57)).
- `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` §4 adds two offline sync rules that the team closed on 2026-11-06. DS-11: the level copy with the higher `version` wins, so a level update reaches players only when it raises `version`. DS-12: the level results table keeps the best result per user, game and level, and the high score keeps the highest value. DS-12 closes the every-attempt question of DS-4; the order that defines the best result stays open in DS-4.

### Fixed

- No `ErrorDto` body holds the query of the request ([#68](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/68)). `HttpErrorFilter` removes each copy of the query from `message` and `details`, as it already did for `path`. Before, `GET /no-such-resource?token=secret-value` answered the 404 `message` `Cannot GET /no-such-resource?token=secret-value`; now it answers `Cannot GET /no-such-resource`. A message that does not quote the query does not change.
- Three follow-ups from the review of the Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 pull requests ([#55](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/55)). The `path` field of the `ErrorDto` body ends at the first `?`, so an error for `GET /no-such-resource?token=secret-value` answers `path` `/no-such-resource`, and a client that logs error bodies no longer logs the query in `path` (gap G2.4); `openapi/openapi.json` says so. The `ProgressRepository` port promises the level results in ascending `levelId` order, compared as text, as the Drizzle adapter reads them; the in-memory double now sorts them the same way, and the port contract checks the order (gap G3.3). The determinism gate restores the environment in an `afterEach`, so a failed test no longer leaves the fake `JWT_SECRET` to the next tests.
- A thrown error no longer writes its message to the server log, because a message can quote a value that the client sent. `LoggingAspect` and `HttpErrorFilter` log the error name, its `code` when the code is a string, and the stack frames without the first line of the stack, which repeats the message (`useCase=<name> outcome=error error=<error name> code=<code>` from the aspect, `error=<error name> code=<code>` from the filter). Both take these fields from `describeThrown()` in `src/application/thrown-value.ts`. A stack that does not start with the name and the message logs no frames, and a thrown value that is not an `Error` logs only its type. A thrown value that the filter cannot read still gets the same 500 answer, without a log line. The `error` entry of the `Logger` port holds `name`, `code` and `frames` in place of `message` and `stack` ([#67](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/67)).
- `UserId.from` rejects a value that is not a UUID with «A UserId must be a UUID.», as the `uuid` columns `users.id` and `progress.user_id` do (G42.2, [#64](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/64)). It accepts the RFC 9562 text form (8-4-4-4-12 hexadecimal digits, any version) in either letter case and keeps the value in lower case, as Postgres returns it, so `equals()` matches an id read back from the database. A blank or non-string value still gets «A UserId needs a value.». A token signed with the right secret whose `sub` is not a UUID now gets the same 401 `Invalid or expired token` as any other bad token and never reaches a repository; before, `GET /auth/me` answered 200 with that `sub` and the progress endpoints answered 500 from a database error. The auth and progress test doubles use fixed UUIDs from `test/support/user-ids.ts`. No HTTP schema change and no migration.
- Seven defects that the test-gap review found ([#51](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/51)): a blank or non-string `UserId` is rejected; the dummy password is hashed when the login use case starts, so an unknown username costs one scrypt check, as a wrong password does; the guard accepts the `bearer` scheme in any letter case (RFC 7235); an invalid clock date gives a 500, not a 400; two overlapping syncs of one user and game keep the highest score: `ProgressRepository.update()` runs the read, the change and the save of a sync under a Postgres advisory lock per user and game, and the rule stays in the domain; `LoggingAspect` writes a `bigint` as text and never lets a logger failure change the result of the use case.
- Two overlapping syncs of one user and game lost the level results of one of them, and a stale sync could move `updatedAt` back. The same lock of `ProgressRepository.update()` makes the second sync read what the first one saved, and the use case reads the clock under that lock ([#51](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/51)).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-06

### Added

- A token guard (API-FR-01): `JwtAuthGuard` calls the `AuthenticateToken` input port, and `AuthenticateTokenUseCase` verifies the bearer token through `TokenIssuer`. `GET /auth/me` is the first guarded route: it answers 200 with the `userId` of the token, or 401 with the `ErrorDto` body when the token is missing, malformed, expired or signed with another key. `openapi/openapi.json` declares the bearer security scheme ([#31](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-api/issues/31)).
